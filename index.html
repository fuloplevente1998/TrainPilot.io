What TrainPilot covers

Workout planning

Create programs, run quick workouts and plan sessions on a training calendar.

Progress tracking

Keep an editable workout journal with training history, personal records and progress views.

Health Connect

Optionally connect permitted fitness and health signals for summaries, recovery context and training guidance.

Distance activities

Use manual distance entry, timing and optional foreground GPS measurement for supported activities.

Private by design

Core workout data is stored on-device. Cloud and health integrations are optional and permission-gated.

Multilingual

Hungarian, English, German and Romanian interfaces are supported.

Privacy

No ads. No data sales.

TrainPilot currently has no advertising and no developer-operated analytics/data-collection server. Optional integrations are controlled by the user.
