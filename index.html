TrainPilot

Plan. Train. Track. Improve.

TrainPilot is an Android workout planner, training journal and progress companion. It combines custom programs, an active workout flow, calendar planning, progress statistics and optional Health Connect integration.

TrainPilot highlights

Workout planning

Create programs, run quick workouts and plan sessions on a training calendar.

Progress tracking

Keep an editable workout journal with training history, personal records and progress views.

Health Connect

Optionally connect permitted health and fitness signals for summaries, recovery context and training guidance.

Distance activities

Track distance activities with manual entry, timing and optional foreground GPS measurement.

Private by design

Core workout data is stored on-device. Cloud and health integrations are optional and permission-gated.

Multilingual

The app supports Hungarian, English, German and Romanian interfaces.

Privacy matters

TrainPilot currently has no advertising, no data sales and no developer-operated analytics/data-collection server. Optional integrations are controlled by the user and documented in the privacy policy.
