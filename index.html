Privacy Policy

How TrainPilot handles workout, health, Google integration, photo and GPS data.

Effective: 5 October 2026. TrainPilot is developed and published by FulTech Studios.

1. Overview

TrainPilot is a local-first Android workout planner, journal and training companion. Core workout data is stored on your device. Google services, Health Connect, Bluetooth/watch features and GPS distance measurement are optional and require separate user action or permission.

TrainPilot currently contains no advertising, does not sell personal data and does not use a developer-operated analytics or data-collection server.

2. Data stored on the device

Depending on the features you use, TrainPilot can store workout history, exercise sets, program definitions, body-weight entries, settings, drafts, training calendar information, workout photos, permitted Health Connect records/summaries and remembered-watch information in app-owned local storage.

3. Health Connect

On supported Android versions, and only after you grant the relevant permissions, TrainPilot can read Health Connect data used for journal summaries, trends, recovery context and training suggestions.

With a separate write permission, TrainPilot can write the name, type, start time and end time of TrainPilot workouts to Health Connect. It does not copy wearable heart-rate or calorie records back as TrainPilot measurements.

4. Google account, Drive and Calendar

Optional Google Drive synchronization uses your own Google account's application-data folder to store TrainPilot backups and, when enabled, related workout photos. Optional Google Calendar synchronization manages calendars/events created for TrainPilot workflows.

Google integrations are optional. You can revoke Google access and remove TrainPilot-managed cloud/calendar data separately from local device data.

5. Health data in backups

Health data is excluded from manual JSON/ZIP backups and Drive synchronization by default. You can explicitly enable health-data inclusion in TrainPilot's data/privacy settings.

6. Photos, GPS and Bluetooth

Workout photos are stored in private app-owned storage. GPS distance tracking starts only when you request it and after Android grants precise location permission (no routes are saved). Bluetooth/watch integration is optional and stores temporary scan identifiers locally to connect.

7. Data deletion and retention

You can erase TrainPilot's local data from within the app. Local erasure removes TrainPilot's local journal, but it cannot automatically remove copies that you exported, cloud data stored in external services or Health Connect records managed by Android.

8. Magyar összefoglaló

A TrainPilot alapvetően helyben tárolja az edzésadataidat. A Health Connect, Google Drive/Naptár, Bluetooth/óra és GPS funkciók opcionálisak. Nincs reklám, adatértékesítés vagy saját külső analitikai/adatgyűjtő szerver. A Health Connectből engedélyezett adatokat az alkalmazás naplóhoz, trendekhez és edzésjavaslatokhoz használhatja. Az alkalmazás nem orvosi diagnosztikai eszköz. A helyi adatok az alkalmazáson belül törölhetők.
