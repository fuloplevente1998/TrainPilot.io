Before contacting support

  • Include your TrainPilot app version and Android version.
  • Describe the screen or feature where the issue occurred.
  • For Health Connect issues, mention the relevant permissions and source apps, but avoid sending health measurements unless they are specifically needed in a private support exchange.
  • For sync issues, state whether the problem involves Google Drive, Google Calendar, Health Connect, Bluetooth/watch data or local backup/restore.

Privacy requests

TrainPilot primarily stores data locally. Use the in-app data/privacy controls to erase local TrainPilot data. Google Drive/Calendar content and Health Connect records are managed through their respective services or Android settings.

Read the full privacy policy.

Do not send health records, passwords, access tokens, backup files or other sensitive information in public messages.
