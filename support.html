Support

Help, privacy questions and account/integration guidance for TrainPilot.

Contact FulTech Studios

A dedicated support email has been created and will be published here before the public Google Play release.

Temporary developer contact: github.com/fuloplevente1998

Do not send or publish health records, passwords, access tokens, backup files or other sensitive personal information in public messages.

Before contacting support

  • Include your TrainPilot app version and Android version.
  • Describe the screen/feature where the issue occurred.
  • For Health Connect issues, mention which permissions and source apps are involved, but do not include actual health measurements unless specifically needed in a private support channel.
  • For sync issues, state whether the problem involves Google Drive, Google Calendar, Health Connect, Bluetooth/watch data or local backup/restore.

Privacy requests

TrainPilot primarily stores data locally. Use the in-app data/privacy controls to erase local TrainPilot data. Google Drive/Calendar content and Health Connect records are managed through their respective services/system settings.
