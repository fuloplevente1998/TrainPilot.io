Privacy policy

How TrainPilot handles workout, health, Google integration, photo and GPS data.

Effective: 5 October 2026. TrainPilot is developed and published by FulTech Studios. This page is intended to be the public privacy-policy URL for the Android application.

1. Overview

TrainPilot is a local-first Android workout planner, journal and training companion. Core workout data is stored on your device. Google services, Health Connect, Bluetooth/watch features and GPS distance measurement are optional and require separate user action or permission.

TrainPilot currently contains no advertising, does not sell personal data and does not use a developer-operated analytics or data-collection server.

2. Data stored on the device

Depending on the features you use, TrainPilot can store workout history, exercise sets, program definitions, body-weight entries, settings, drafts, training calendar information, workout photos, permitted Health Connect records/summaries and remembered-watch information in app-owned local storage.

Android automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for TrainPilot app data. Explicit exports and optional integrations described below remain available.

3. Health Connect

On supported Android versions, and only after you grant the relevant permissions, TrainPilot can read Health Connect data used for journal summaries, trends, recovery context and training suggestions. Depending on granted permissions, this may include exercise, active and total calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep, heart-rate variability (HRV), steps, weight, body fat, oxygen saturation, VO₂ max, distance, speed, blood pressure, blood glucose and respiratory rate.

TrainPilot may store permitted Health Connect records and source metadata in a private native SQLite journal on the device. Overlapping activity sources are not blindly added together. Health-related scores, calorie values and Coach guidance are fitness estimates and are not medical diagnoses.

With a separate write permission, TrainPilot can write the name, type, start time and end time of TrainPilot workouts to Health Connect. It does not copy wearable heart-rate or calorie records back as TrainPilot measurements.

Optional Health Connect background refresh requires separate system permission and a supported Android version. You can revoke Health Connect permissions or remove Health Connect records through Android system settings.

4. Google account, Drive and Calendar

If you choose to connect Google, TrainPilot stores your Google account identifier, email address and name locally for the integration. Access tokens are not exposed to the app's JavaScript data model, backups or application logs.

Optional Google Drive synchronization uses your own Google account's application-data folder to store TrainPilot backups and, when enabled, related workout photos. Optional Google Calendar synchronization manages calendars/events created for TrainPilot workflows.

Google integrations are optional. You can revoke Google access and remove TrainPilot-managed cloud/calendar data separately from local device data.

5. Health data in backups

Health data is excluded from manual JSON/ZIP backups and Drive synchronization by default. You can explicitly enable health-data inclusion in TrainPilot's data/privacy settings. When enabled, eligible workout health summaries, recovery history and manual health-journal exports may be included.

Turning this option off does not erase exports or cloud backups that you previously created. A backup file you share with another person may contain personal information.

6. Photos

Workout photos are stored in private app-owned storage. A full ZIP backup can include referenced photo files. TrainPilot's photo workflow is designed not to retain the original image's location/EXIF metadata after the app processes the image.

7. GPS distance measurement

GPS distance tracking starts only when you request it and after Android grants precise location permission. TrainPilot can use a foreground location service so measurement can continue while the screen is locked.

TrainPilot does not store a GPS route or route coordinates as workout history. The feature stores the resulting estimated aggregate distance and elapsed time. Manual distance entry can be used without location permission.

8. Bluetooth and remembered watch

Bluetooth/watch integration is optional. Temporary Bluetooth scan identifiers are used locally to connect to a selected compatible device. If you explicitly remember a watch, TrainPilot stores the information required to reconnect. Diagnostic exports are designed to omit raw Bluetooth addresses and packet data.

9. Data deletion and retention

You can erase TrainPilot's local data from within the app. Local erasure removes TrainPilot's local journal and app-managed local settings, but it cannot automatically remove copies that you exported, cloud data stored in external services or Health Connect records managed by Android. Those external copies must be removed through the corresponding service or system settings.

If synchronization is active on another device, disable it there as well to prevent data from being uploaded again.

10. Third-party services

Optional features can interact with Android Health Connect and Google services such as Google Drive and Google Calendar. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies. TrainPilot only requests access needed for enabled user-facing features.

11. Children and medical use

TrainPilot is a fitness and workout application, not a medical device and not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Magyar összefoglaló

A TrainPilot alapvetően helyben tárolja az edzésadataidat. A Health Connect, Google Drive/Naptár, Bluetooth/óra és GPS funkciók opcionálisak, külön engedélyhez vagy bekapcsoláshoz kötöttek. Nincs reklám, adatértékesítés vagy saját külső analitikai/adatgyűjtő szerver. A Health Connectből engedélyezett adatokat az alkalmazás naplóhoz, trendekhez és edzésjavaslatokhoz használhatja. Az alkalmazás nem orvosi diagnosztikai eszköz.

A helyi adatok az alkalmazáson belül törölhetők. A korábban exportált fájlokat, Google-felhőadatokat és Health Connect-bejegyzéseket külön, az adott szolgáltatásban kell törölni.
