TRAINPILOT · PRIVACY

Privacy policy

How TrainPilot handles workout, health, Google integration, photo and GPS data.

Effective: 5 October 2026. TrainPilot is developed and published by FulTech Studios.

1. Overview

TrainPilot is a local-first Android workout planner, journal and training companion. Core workout data is stored on your device. Google services, Health Connect, Bluetooth/watch features and GPS distance measurement are optional and require separate user action or permission.

TrainPilot currently contains no advertising, does not sell personal data and does not use a developer-operated analytics or data-collection server.

2. Data stored on the device

Depending on the features you use, TrainPilot can store workout history, exercise sets, program definitions, body-weight entries, settings, drafts, training calendar information, workout photos, permitted Health Connect records/summaries and remembered-watch information in app-owned local storage.

Android automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for TrainPilot app data. Explicit exports and optional integrations described below remain available.

3. Health Connect

On supported Android versions, and only after you grant the relevant permissions, TrainPilot can read permitted Health Connect data used for journal summaries, trends, recovery context and training suggestions.

TrainPilot may store permitted Health Connect records and source metadata in a private native journal on the device. Overlapping activity sources are not blindly added together. Health-related scores, calorie values and Coach guidance are fitness estimates and are not medical diagnoses.

With separate permission, TrainPilot can write TrainPilot workout sessions to Health Connect. Optional background refresh also requires separate system permission and a supported Android version.

4. Google account, Drive and Calendar

If you choose to connect Google, TrainPilot stores the account information required for the integration locally. Access tokens are not exposed to the app's JavaScript data model, backups or application logs.

Optional Google Drive synchronization uses your own Google account's application-data folder for TrainPilot backups. Optional Google Calendar synchronization manages calendars/events created for TrainPilot workflows.

5. Health data in backups

Health data is excluded from manual backups and Drive synchronization by default. You can explicitly enable health-data inclusion in TrainPilot's data/privacy settings. Disabling the option later does not erase backups you previously created.

6. Photos

Workout photos are stored in private app-owned storage. A full ZIP backup can include referenced photo files. TrainPilot's photo workflow is designed not to retain the original image's location/EXIF metadata after processing.

7. GPS distance measurement

GPS distance tracking starts only when you request it and after Android grants precise location permission. TrainPilot can use a foreground location service so measurement can continue while the screen is locked.

TrainPilot stores the resulting estimated aggregate distance and elapsed time rather than a workout route history. Manual distance entry can be used without location permission.

8. Bluetooth and remembered watch

Bluetooth/watch integration is optional. Temporary scan identifiers are used locally to connect to a selected compatible device. If you explicitly remember a watch, TrainPilot stores the information required to reconnect.

9. Data deletion and retention

You can erase TrainPilot's local data from within the app. This cannot automatically remove copies you exported, cloud data stored in external services or Health Connect records managed by Android. Those external copies must be removed through the corresponding service or system settings.

10. Third-party services

Optional features can interact with Android Health Connect and Google services such as Google Drive and Google Calendar. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Children and medical use

TrainPilot is a fitness and workout application, not a medical device and not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
